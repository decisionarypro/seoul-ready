OPTION C

Phone doesn't support eSIM?

Consider an airport physical SIM, pocket Wi‑Fi, or roaming from your home carrier. An eSIM won't fix device incompatibility.

A FEW GOOD QUESTIONS

Before you
take off.

Do I have to book through this site?+

No. Seoul Ready is an independent informational guide. When you choose a provider, you go to their official website and complete any purchase directly with them.

Are these the cheapest options?+

Not necessarily. We avoid making price promises because plans, exchange rates and promotions change. Compare the final price and what's included before paying.

Will a travel eSIM give me a Korean phone number?+

Not always. Many travel eSIMs are data-only. If you need voice calls or text messages from a Korean number, read the plan details carefully and compare Korean local carrier options.
